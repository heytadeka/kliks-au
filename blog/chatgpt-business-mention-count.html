ChatGPT Is Testing a Mention Counter. Here's What Shopify Stores Should Do.

Adam Nagy 9 November 2026 4 min read

OpenAI is reportedly testing a dashboard that would show a business how often ChatGPT mentions it and how many clicks those mentions send to its website. It has not launched and OpenAI hasn't confirmed it. Everything known so far comes from a screenshot and unnamed sources in a Digiday report published on 9 November 2026. For a Shopify store, the sensible move now is not to buy anything. It's to find out what ChatGPT already says about you and make sure your product and business information is accurate.

Juozas Kaziukėnas, founder of Marketplace Pulse, posted a screenshot on LinkedIn on 6 November of a banner reading "Claim your business in ChatGPT". He says he first saw traces of it in August. Digiday cites two sources with knowledge of how it works. OpenAI declined to comment, and at the time of writing there was no announcement from the company.

What does the ChatGPT business dashboard reportedly show?

According to Kaziukėnas, it charts impressions and clicks per day. In plain terms, an impression is a ChatGPT answer that included your business, and a click is a visit to your site from one. Prompts and user data stay hidden, and there is no competitor ranking. Claiming would also let you keep product and business details current and unlock what OpenAI calls a business agent, which answers customer questions.

Why does the Shopify connection matter?

Claiming reportedly needs proof that you can represent the business, and Kaziukėnas says connecting a Shopify store is one way to give it. If that holds, the product titles, descriptions, prices and business details in your Shopify admin are the information ChatGPT could draw on. That part is my inference, not something OpenAI has said. It still points to a cheap job worth doing now: make those details accurate, and make sure they match your website, Google Business Profile and social profiles. It helps real customers whether or not ChatGPT ever uses it.

Who actually benefits from a ChatGPT mention counter?

Digiday's view is that the dashboard deals in organic answers and sits apart from ChatGPT ads, but OpenAI's ads business has the most to gain. Once visibility is a daily number, owners will want it to climb, and the lever OpenAI charges for is a paid placement beside the answer. Google ran the same play with Business Profile and Search Console. According to Digiday, OpenAI claims a $1 billion annualised ads run rate by September and says more than 100,000 advertisers had run campaigns across 89 countries by late October. Ads began testing in the US in February. Nothing reported says when, or whether, any of this reaches Australia.

I run paid ads for a living, so I'll be blunt. A mention count is a number to watch, not a result to pay for. Mentions aren't sales, and nobody outside OpenAI can promise what moves them.

What can a Shopify store sensibly do now?

Ask ChatGPT, Claude and Perplexity the questions your customers would ask, in their words. Note whether your store appears, what each says about it, and who comes up instead. Ask a few times, because answers vary. Then check that your product pages, About page, shipping and returns information and Google Business Profile all agree on what you sell, where you ship and what it costs.

Then hold your wallet. GEO vendors such as Profound and AdthenaHQ sell visibility tracking by running prompts through ChatGPT at scale. Kaziukėnas expects them to complement OpenAI's counts, not be replaced by them, since the dashboard reportedly leaves out the prompts and competitor rankings they sell. Until you've seen what the first-party version covers, a yearly tracking contract is hard to justify.

Short version: this is a test, not a launch. Check what ChatGPT says about your store, tidy your product and business information, and wait to see what OpenAI's own numbers cover before paying for tracking.

Frequently asked questions

Has OpenAI launched the ChatGPT Business Hub?

No. It is an unreleased feature that Digiday reports OpenAI is testing, based on a screenshot and two sources. OpenAI declined to comment, and no announcement had been published at the time of writing.

Can I claim my business in ChatGPT yet?

Nothing confirmed says so. The claim banner appeared in a screenshot of an unreleased feature, and OpenAI hasn't said how claiming will work or when it will open.

Will it show how I rank against competitors?

Not in the version described. It reportedly shows daily impressions and clicks only, with prompts, user data and competitor rankings hidden. What OpenAI eventually ships could differ.
