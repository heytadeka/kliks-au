04What's missing

The site tells people Mactrans is reliable. Nothing shows it.

— 01
No face behind the brand
22 years in business, and the About and Contact pages name no one, no founder story, no team photo. Corporate buyers commit to relationships, not just fleets.
— 02
No visible client trust signals
No testimonials, no case studies, no client logos anywhere on-site, despite serving "leading corporations" for two decades, per the site's own copy.
— 03
No content beyond the core service pages
No blog, no resource content, nothing for Google or an AI assistant to actually cite when someone searches the category.
— 04
Zero Google reviews visible
The Google Business Profile is claimed and verified, but shows no review count and no rating at all.
— 05
No paid presence anywhere
No active Facebook or Instagram ads, no Google Ads. Every competitor named above is winning the searches Mactrans isn't even contesting.
— 06
No lead capture
No email capture, no way to follow up with a visitor who isn't ready to call today. Every non-converting visit is gone for good.
