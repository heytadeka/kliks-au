What you can realistically expect

We don't promise miracles. But we do promise these.

We're a team of ad junkies, so here's what you can count on. Concrete metrics we work to improve, measurable proof it's working.

More revenue from your ad spend
ROAS / POAS ↑

ROAS is revenue per dollar spent on ads. POAS is profit per dollar spent. We track both, because revenue alone isn't enough. Profitable scaling is the goal.

More customers, same budget
CPA ↓ — cost per acquisition

CPA is what you pay to acquire each customer. We bring it down through better campaign structure, creative rotation and audience targeting.

Stop burning your audience
Reach:Frequency ⚖

If the same person sees your ad too many times, it gets more expensive and performs worse. We monitor this daily and rotate creative before it becomes a problem.

More clicks that actually convert
CTR + Conversion ↑

CTR (click-through rate) tells us if the ad is working. Conversion rate tells us if the store is. We optimise both ends so clicks turn into customers.

01 - Tip

What to ask every marketing agency before you sign anything

One question that filters out 90% of agencies:

"Do they run their own businesses?"

If not, why would you listen to them?

If someone has never risked their own money on an ad, never watched a campaign fail, never had to explain why revenue fell short — what gives them the right to tell you how to grow your business?

Most agencies sell theory. Borrowed frameworks, never tested with anything at stake. Then they send the invoice.

The follow-up question:

Can they show you a failure?

Anyone can show a case study. Just pick your best client's best month. But an agency that can show a failure, explain what went wrong, and what they learned — that's an agency actually doing the work.

Anyone who's never made a mistake is either lying or hasn't really tried anything.
