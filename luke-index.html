07 FAQ

QUESTIONS
ANSWERED.

The most common things people ask before applying. If yours isn't here, send me a message.

Do I need a gym membership?
+
No. Programmes can be built around a gym, home equipment, or a combination of both. When you apply just let me know what you have access to and we'll build around it.
How much time do I need to commit per week?
+
Most clients train 3 to 5 days per week. Sessions are typically 45 to 60 minutes. The programme is designed around your schedule so if you only have 3 days available, we build for 3 days.
What does nutrition coaching actually look like?
+
No rigid meal plans. Instead we work out your targets, build a flexible framework that fits your food preferences and lifestyle, and adjust it as we go based on how your body responds and how you're feeling.
How long until I see results?
+
Most clients notice meaningful changes within the first 4 to 6 weeks. Visible body composition changes typically come at the 8 to 12 week mark depending on starting point and consistency. The minimum commitment is 12 weeks for a reason.
How do check-ins work?
+
Weekly check-ins via a short form — progress photos, weight, energy levels, how training felt. I review everything and adjust your programme accordingly. You also have direct message access to me throughout the week for any questions that come up.
