01 The opportunity
🏆
La Liste Australian Top 50 — 2024
The only vegan bakery on the list. International recognition.
"An accolade this significant deserves to be front and centre on your site, your socials, your packaging, and every ad you run."

The ingredients are world-class.
The digital presence isn't there yet.

Having worked with Oh My Days before, we know the product, the audience, and the story intimately. Here is where the digital side has room to catch up.

01
Reviews left unanswered
Google, Yelp, and TripAdvisor reviews sitting without a response. Unanswered reviews, positive or negative, signal to new customers that nobody is home. A systematic response strategy builds trust and lifts local SEO ranking at the same time.
02
No review collection strategy
Reviews are coming in organically but there's no system to collect them actively. A simple post-purchase email flow and in-store QR prompt could double your review volume within 60 days, without asking customers awkwardly.
03
Site feels template-driven
The website works but it doesn't feel like Oh My Days. Generic icons, no allergen filtering, no collection feature cards, no founder presence. Customers who land from an ad need more than a product grid to convert.
04
No paid acquisition
14K followers and La Liste recognition with no performance marketing behind it. Google Shopping, Meta retargeting, and TikTok organic are all untapped, each one a direct pipeline to new customers who don't yet know you exist.
05
Content without a calendar
Great product, inconsistent output. A monthly shoot day and a structured content calendar turns one day of effort into 30 days of presence across Instagram, TikTok, and Google.
06
Founder story untold
David's fine dining background at Bentley and est. is a genuine differentiator. It doesn't need to be a big production. Even a single well-chosen photo of David in the kitchen, paired with a few honest lines about why he started Oh My Days, is enough. People buy from people. Right now that story isn't on the site.
